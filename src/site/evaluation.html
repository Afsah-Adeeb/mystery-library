03 · ANSWERS

Honest about the gaps.

Answers scored against facts checked by script to appear in the book. Measured on plain retrieval, the baseline the corrective flow is built to beat.

It never invented an answer to a trick question. The weakness is the other direction: it often finds the right book but not the exact passage, then declines a question it could have answered. That is the case the corrective step's “search deeper” exists for. The head-to-head comparison of plain and corrective retrieval ran out of free API quota; it re-runs on the next reset.
